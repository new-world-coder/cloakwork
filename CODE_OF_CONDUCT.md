# Code of Conduct

CloakWork is an open-source privacy project. People contributing code, tests, docs, and reviews are welcome, including first-time contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful. Critique the change, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when a design choice is unclear.
- Keep discussions public in issues and pull requests so the next contributor can follow them.
- Give feedback that points at a file, a test, or a concrete next step.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or disclosing someone's private information.
- Pushing the project toward fraud, account abuse, or hidden bypasses of a named site. Consistency and leak detection are in scope. Evasion playbooks are not.
- Posting exploit procedures, stolen data, or other people's credentials.

## Reporting

Until a public issue tracker is attached, contact the maintainers through the channel they publish on the project site. Do not file conduct reports as public good-first-issue threads.

Maintainers will respond, may remove comments or code that break this policy, and may turn away contributions that conflict with it.
